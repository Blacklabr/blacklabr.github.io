BlackLabr follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit the website. The information collected may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used for analyzing trends, administering the site, and gathering demographic information.

Like most websites, blacklabr.com uses “cookies”. These cookies store information including visitors’ preferences and the pages that were accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing content. You can disable cookies through your individual browser settings.
