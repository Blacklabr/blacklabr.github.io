Fishing App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026 · Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. Who We Are

Fishing App (the “App”) is the iOS app operated by BlackLabr Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”), an online company registered in New Zealand and the data controller for the App. This Policy explains, in plain terms, how the App handles your information. Questions? Contact support@blacklabr.com. See also our Terms and company Privacy Policy.

2. Your Account

You do not need to register. The App creates an account automatically so it can save and synchronise your records — you are not asked for a name, email address or password. A unique identifier links your records to your account.

3. What We Collect

We collect only what the App needs to work, and permission-based data only when you use the related feature:

  • Your fishing records — catches, notes and saved places, with their dates, times and locations. These are private and not shown to other users.
  • Location — with your permission, to show your position, log catches and choose forecasts.
  • Photos — with your permission; photo files stay on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Technical, usage and diagnostic data — to run the App and fix problems. This is not linked to your account and is not used to identify you.
  • Notifications — if you turn them on.
  • Advertising identifier — only if you allow it (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency), and used solely to measure which campaign led to your install. Not used to show you ads.
  • Support messages — if you email us.

4. How We Use and Share It

We use this information to provide, secure and improve the App. We rely on service providers — for hosting and sync, maps and place search, weather and tide data, and analytics — who process it on our behalf and only for those purposes.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: your consent for permission-based data (location, photos, notifications, and the advertising identifier); performance of our agreement with you to run your account and sync your records; and our legitimate interests to keep the App secure and to diagnose and improve it. You can withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect processing already carried out.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and fishing records for as long as your account exists, so the App can show your history — we do not delete it just because you have been away. Technical, usage and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period and then rotated out. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle and follow up your request.

You can remove individual catches or places, or delete your whole account with Settings → Delete My Data, which removes your account and its data from our servers and clears the App’s data from your phone. Copies may remain briefly in routine backups until they expire on the normal backup cycle. Photos you saved to your own library and emails you sent to support are not affected.

7. International Transfers

We are based in New Zealand, and our service providers may process your data in other countries, including the United States and the EU. Where they do, we take steps to keep your data protected to a standard consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

8. Your Rights

You can access, correct or delete your information — use Delete My Data in the App, or email support@blacklabr.com. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights:

  • EU/UK (GDPR): access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): know what we collect and delete it. If we use an advertising identifier for install attribution, that may count as “sharing”; you can opt out by declining tracking in iOS or contacting us. We do not sell your data.

We aim to respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and reputable infrastructure providers to protect your information. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will address issues promptly. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and any relevant authority without undue delay, as required by law.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and Contact

We will post changes to this Policy on this page and update the date above; for material changes we will give notice in the App or by other appropriate means.
