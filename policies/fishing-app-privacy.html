Fishing App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026 · Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Who We Are

Fishing App (the “App”) is operated by BlackLabr Limited (“BlackLabr”, “we”, “us”, “our”), registered in New Zealand. This Policy applies to the App and its related services.

BlackLabr Limited is the data controller for individual users. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. Terms not otherwise defined here have the meanings set out in our Terms and Conditions. For information about our website and company, see our company Privacy Policy.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account Data — name, email, login credentials.
  • Location (GPS) — to show nearby waters, weather and conditions, and to let you save fishing spots.
  • Camera / Photos — to add photos of your catch and spots.
  • Payment Data — we don’t store card numbers. Processed by app store platforms and payment processors.
  • Push Notification Token — to send notifications you opt into (e.g. bite times, weather alerts).
  • Advertising / Attribution ID — IDFA (iOS, via ATT permission) and GAID (Android).
  • Support Communications — messages and attachments via in-app chat/helpdesk.
  • Usage / Technical Data — IP, device/OS, app version, activity logs, crash and diagnostic data.
  • Cookies (Web) — session, preference, and analytics.

All permission-based data (location, camera, push, advertising ID) is collected only when the related feature is used by you, for core app functionality, and is not sold or shared with advertisers beyond the attribution use stated above.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS). We request permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Account data — contract performance
  • Location — consent
  • Camera / photos — consent
  • Payment processing — contract performance
  • Push token — consent (notification permission)
  • Advertising ID — consent (ATT prompt on iOS; opt-out available)
  • Support chat data — contract performance / legitimate interests
  • Usage / technical data — legitimate interests
  • Marketing emails — consent

You may withdraw consent at any time; this doesn’t affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Billing platforms — subscription processing (app stores and web)
  • Analytics providers — usage tracking, crash reporting
  • Advertising / attribution networks — ad campaign measurement, install attribution
  • Support / helpdesk platform — customer support ticketing and chat
  • Cloud hosting providers — data storage and infrastructure

Providers may change over time. A current list of active sub-processors is available on request (see Section 12). We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data — until account deletion
  • Saved spots and location history — until deleted by user or account deletion
  • Catch photos — until deleted by user or account deletion
  • Support chat history — 24 months, or per helpdesk provider policy
  • Usage logs and diagnostics — 12 months rolling
  • Payment records — as required by applicable tax law (typically 7 years)

To delete your account and associated data, contact us (Section 12) or use the in-app account deletion feature.

6. International Data Transfers

BlackLabr Limited is based in New Zealand. Your data may be processed in other countries, including the United States and EU member states, through our sub-processors. Where we transfer data from the EU/UK, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or applicable adequacy decisions.

7. Your Rights

All users: access, correct, or delete your data; withdraw consent.

EU/UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): restrict or object to processing; data portability; lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): know what’s collected and how it’s used; request deletion; opt out of “sale or sharing.” We use advertising identifiers for campaign attribution, which may qualify as “sharing” under CCPA/CPRA. Opt out by disabling tracking permissions on your device, or by contacting us (Section 12).

Other jurisdictions: we comply with applicable laws in Canada (PIPEDA), Brazil (LGPD), Australia, New Zealand, and other markets where we operate.

To exercise any right, contact us using the details in Section 12. We respond within 30 days and may require identity verification.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact us if you believe we have, and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is 100% secure.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant supervisory authorities without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours where required by GDPR.

11. Changes to This Policy

Changes are posted here with an updated effective date. For material changes, we notify users via email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.
